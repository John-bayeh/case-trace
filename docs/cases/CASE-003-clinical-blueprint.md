# CASE-003 Clinical Blueprint

**The Result That Never Reached the Follow-Up Visit** is a synthetic pathology
workflow investigation. The patient has iron-deficiency anemia, undergoes
upper endoscopy, and receives a gastric biopsy result with intestinal
metaplasia and focal indefinite atypia. The result is finalized and routed,
but the record does not establish patient discussion or timely completed
follow-up.

The evidence is distributed across the consultation, procedure and specimen
records, pathology report, routing record, administrative closure, primary
care note, referral scheduling, and delayed specialist review. The case does
not establish negligence, missed communication, malignancy, or progression.
The key uncertainty is whether undocumented communication occurred before the
later documented visit.

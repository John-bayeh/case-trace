# CASE-004 Clinical Blueprint

**The Diagnosis That Explained Too Little** challenges an anxiety working
diagnosis with objective ambulatory-monitor evidence of paroxysmal
supraventricular tachycardia. The record supports reconsidering an anxiety-only
explanation but does not prove that anxiety is absent or establish a definitive
alternative diagnosis. Thyroid, electrolyte, and blood-count results provide
context and competing explanations.

The intended investigation connects recurring symptoms, the original working
diagnosis, diagnostic orders, monitor interpretation, later reassessment, and
specialist referral. Further rhythm evaluation remains an unresolved clinical
question.

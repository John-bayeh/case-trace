# CASE-005 Clinical Blueprint

**The Missing Days of Anticoagulation** follows a patient with atrial
fibrillation taking apixaban through a procedure. A hold instruction, discharge
medication list, later reconciliation, and patient report disagree about the
hold and restart interval. Prescription status does not establish dispensing,
administration, or ingestion.

The intended finding is a documented anticoagulation timeline discrepancy with
uncertain actual exposure. The record does not establish that an adverse event
was caused by interruption and does not provide dosing advice.

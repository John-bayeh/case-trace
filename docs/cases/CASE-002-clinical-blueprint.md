# CASE-002 Clinical Blueprint

## Title

**The Medication List That Never Caught Up**

CASE-002 is a synthetic longitudinal medication-reconciliation investigation. A
patient with hypertension and reduced baseline kidney function has a February
prescribing change from lisinopril to losartan. Later emergency and primary-care
records retain or report lisinopril, but no record proves when the patient last
took it. Renal function worsens during a diarrheal illness and reported
non-prescription ibuprofen exposure, then partially improves.

## Intended discoveries

1. The prescribing record, outside medication list, and patient-reported bottle
   create a verified documentation discrepancy.
2. Creatinine, eGFR, potassium, and BUN show an acute worsening followed by
   partial recovery.
3. The record supports possible medication exposure but does not establish
   dispensing, ingestion, or medication-induced kidney injury.

## Clinical guardrails

The case does not claim a definitive adverse drug reaction. An order is not
dispensing, a medication list is not administration, and patient recollection
does not provide exact ingestion dates. The record intentionally preserves
volume depletion and ibuprofen exposure as plausible non-medication contributors.

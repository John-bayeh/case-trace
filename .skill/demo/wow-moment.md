# The Wow Moment: Evidence-Based Timeline Contradiction

## Goal
Convert a fragmented medical history into a comprehensible, surprising discrepancy supported by original source material. The reveal must be earned by the agent's actual work.

## Narrative pattern
**Before:** each visit looks routine in isolation. **Discovery:** combining records exposes two descriptions of a consequential event that cannot both be true *under the same interpretation*. **Verification:** the agent pulls both originals, checks timestamp semantics/amendments, and explains why the contradiction matters. **After:** a clear evidence-linked report gives an actionable documentation-review question, not a medical verdict.

## On-screen choreography
1. At first, show an unannotated longitudinal record view.
2. Reveal the evolving timeline as real tool responses arrive.
3. Highlight the suspect interval only once a candidate contradiction exists.
4. Place source A and source B in a split view with timestamp, encounter, author/source type, and cited text/field highlighted.
5. Visually connect timeline nodes to their originals; one click opens exact cited context.
6. Explain in plain language: what record A says; what B says; why they disagree; what remains uncertain.
7. End with report and a quick live citation click-through.

## Credibility checks
- Cite at least two existing source records with resolvable immutable identifiers.
- Distinguish `event_time`, `documented_time`, `signed_time` and `amended_time` where present.
- Do not treat delayed charting as automatically clinically impossible.
- Do not infer patient harm or make treatment recommendations unsupported by data.
- Preserve conflicts visibly; do not silently select one record as true.

## Design guidance
Use strong visual hierarchy, synchronized timeline/evidence selection, minimal animations, prominent but accurate provenance metadata, and accessible keyboard controls. Do not make visual prominence imply medical certainty.

## Validation
A judge can explain the contradiction in one sentence and open both source documents without trusting the model's word alone. A skeptical reviewer can locate the uncertainty notes and any tested competing explanations.
